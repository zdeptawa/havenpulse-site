Know when something changes, even when you're not there.

Haven Pulse is building simple tools for monitoring the places and conditions that matter — and letting you know when something needs your attention.

In development

Remote monitoring and awareness

We're developing a platform that brings together external alerts, such as severe weather, with remote monitoring of environmental conditions, connectivity, and power status.

Whether it's home, a remote property, or somewhere you help look after, the goal is simple: give you useful awareness without requiring you to constantly check it yourself.

Built around real-world monitoring

Haven Pulse is currently being prototyped and tested with real-world alerting and remote-property monitoring scenarios. We're focused on making monitoring straightforward, useful, and accessible.
